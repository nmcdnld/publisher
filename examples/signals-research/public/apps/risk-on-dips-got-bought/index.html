In a risk-on tape, breakdowns got bought and big gaps faded

With SPY above its 200-day, a close below the prior 20-session low on the 27 stocks was followed by a five-session return +2.29 points above base (t 2.9, 206 firings). Gaps of 3% or more reverted both ways: +1.42 after a gap down, −1.13 after a gap up.
